About AIAD

Artist Intelligence & Development

AIAD is a platform built to give independent artists the kind of tools that used to belong only to major labels — powered by AI, and priced for people building their careers on their own terms.

What we do

We put an artist's whole operation in one place, with an AI layer on top that actually helps:

  • AIAD Agent — an AI career partner that reads your real numbers and gives specific, strategic advice: what to release, how to price, where to focus.
  • Analytics & Stats — your streaming, social and audience data pulled together and made legible, so you can see what's working without a spreadsheet.
  • Store & Payouts — sell music, merch and digital goods directly to fans, keep the vast majority of every sale, and get paid out cleanly.
  • Supporters — a direct line to the fans who back you, without an algorithm deciding who hears from you.
  • Collaborators — find and work with other artists, producers and creatives by genre and fit.

Who it's for

Independent musicians and creators who are serious about their craft and tired of guessing. Whether you're just starting or already touring, AIAD meets you where you are and grows with you.

Our mission

To level the playing field. Great artists shouldn't need a label's back office to make smart decisions — they need clear information and honest tools. That's what we're building.
