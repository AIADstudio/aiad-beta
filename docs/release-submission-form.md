# AIAD Release Submission Form

Everything AIAD needs to put a release on the Streaming Layer. Same information any distributor asks for — nothing extra. Fields marked ● are required. A release cannot go live with any ● field empty.

## SECTION 1 — Release information
● Release title — as it should appear. No ALL CAPS unless stylized that way, no "(Official Audio)", no featured artists in the title.
Version / subtitle — e.g. Deluxe, Acoustic, Radio Edit, Remastered 2026.
● Release type — Single (1–3 tracks), EP (4–6), Album (7+).
● Primary artist — must match your AIAD artist profile exactly.
Featured artists — listed separately, not typed into the title.
● Label name — defaults to your artist name if you're independent.
UPC — leave blank and AIAD assigns one free.
● Original release date — the date it first came out anywhere. If unreleased, same as go-live.
● AIAD go-live date — minimum 3 business days after submission for QC.
Pre-save / early access window — Tier 2+ supporters get it up to 72h before public. Choose 0 / 24h / 48h / 72h.
● Primary genre. Secondary genre.
● Language of release.
● Parental advisory — Explicit / Clean / Not applicable.
● Territory — Worldwide (default) or list excluded countries.
● ℗ owner — the recording. Who owns the audio file itself. Entered as a year and
  a name; stored as "℗ 2026 [owner name]".
● © owner — the song. Who wrote the melody and lyrics. Defaults to the same
  year and name as ℗; untick "Same as ℗" when they differ.
● Release artwork — see Delivery Specs.

## SECTION 2 — Per track
Repeat for every track on the release.
● Track number. ● Track title. Version / subtitle.
ISRC — leave blank and AIAD assigns one free. Never reuse an ISRC across different recordings.
● Primary artist(s). Featured artist(s).
● Explicit — Yes / No.
● Language of lyrics — or "Instrumental".
Lyrics — plain text, no timestamps. Displayed in-app.
Preview start point — where the 60s clip begins. Defaults to 0:30.
● Audio file — see Delivery Specs.

### Track credits
● Songwriter(s) — required — Legal name and Split %.
Publisher, performing rights organisation and IPI/CAE are optional. Supply them if you
have them: they help royalties match faster. Not having them does not hold up a release,
and "Not sure / none" is a valid answer for the PRO.
● Producer(s) — required — Name, Role (produced / co-produced / additional).
Mixing engineer — optional. Mastering engineer — optional.
Featured performers — optional — instrument / role.

Songwriter splits must total exactly 100%. Submission is blocked otherwise — that is the one credit rule that is enforced, because splits that do not add up become disputes over money. If you're the sole writer, that's 100% to you. Publisher defaults to "Self-published". If you're not affiliated with a PRO, choose "Not sure / none" — you can add one later, and it is worth doing, because that's money you're leaving on the table.

## SECTION 3 — Rights and clearances
Answer for every track.

● Do you own or exclusively control the master recording? Yes / No — if No, submission stops here. Upload the license or authorization from the owner.

● Is this recording subject to an exclusive agreement with a label, distributor, or aggregator that would prevent a non-exclusive license? No / Yes — if Yes, upload written consent from that party.

● Does this recording contain a sample or interpolation of another recording or composition? No / Yes — if Yes, upload the signed clearance. Required before go-live. "I'll get it later" is not accepted.

● Is this recording a cover of a song you did not write? No / Yes — if Yes, identify the original songwriter(s) and original publisher(s). AIAD covers the mechanical license through the MLC blanket.

● Origin declaration — pick one per track:
  Human-created — no generative AI used in the master
  AI-assisted — generative AI used in production, but composition and performance are human
  AI-generated — the master was generated by a model
This is locked after submission and displayed on the track. Only Human-created tracks are eligible for the AIAD Sync Library. A false declaration is grounds for removal from both Streaming and Sync.

● Does any track contain content that is unlawful, defamatory, or that incites violence or hatred against a protected group? No.

## SECTION 4 — Payee and tax
● Legal name or entity name — must match your tax documents.
● Entity type — Individual / Sole proprietor / LLC / Corporation.
● Country of tax residence.
● Tax form — W-9 (US) or W-8BEN / W-8BEN-E (non-US), collected through Stripe during payout setup.
● Payout account — connected Stripe account.
Payouts cannot be released without a completed tax form. This is a legal requirement, not an AIAD policy.

## SECTION 5 — Files to upload
Master audio — one file per track.
Release artwork — one file.
Sample / interpolation clearances — if applicable.
Split sheet, signed by all writers — required for any track with more than one writer.
Master ownership authorization — only if you answered "No" in Section 3.

## SECTION 6 — Declaration
I confirm that the information in this submission is accurate and complete, that I own or control all rights necessary to grant AIAD the license in the AIAD Streaming License Agreement, that all samples and interpolations are cleared, and that my origin declaration is truthful. I understand AIAD relies on these statements and that false statements may result in removal of my catalog and termination of my account.
● Full legal name. ● Date. ● I agree.

## What happens next
1. Submitted — you get a confirmation with a submission ID.
2. QC review — 1–3 business days. AIAD checks audio, artwork, metadata, splits, and clearances.
3. Approved or Changes requested — if something fails, you get the specific reason and the specific fix, not a generic rejection.
4. Live on your go-live date.

Rejections are almost always one of four things: artwork under 3000×3000, splits that don't total 100%, a missing sample clearance, or an ISRC reused from a different recording.
